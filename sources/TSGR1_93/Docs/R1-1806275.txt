Discussion on TB-to-CW mapping

As mentioned in section 1, for transmission with 2 enabled transport blocks, it’s agreed to use a fixed mapping between transport blocks and codewords. It’s also agreed that, for 1-TB transmission, CW0 should always be used. However, the agreement on TB-to-CW mapping was not captured in current specification.
