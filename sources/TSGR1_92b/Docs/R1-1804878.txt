Introduction

In the last meeting, RAN1 has discussed remaining issues for RAN1 specs and updated the RAN1 specs. In this contribution, we discuss remaining issues on a) ordering of DMRS ports and b) TB to CW mapping, respectively.

Ordering of DMRS ports

At RAN1#92, the following agreement was made for DMRS port ordering [1].

AgreementFor NR Rel-15, DMRS port ordering is performed as follows for one-CW transmission (DL)Simple ordering with increasing index (port number)

In addition to the agreement for one-CW case, we need to conclude two-CW transmission case to complete Rel-15 specification. In [2], additional complexity and/or performance loss are concerned for a certain scenario with multi-TRP/panel transmission while such issue is not induced for the scenario with single-TRP/panel. At this late stage of Rel-15 timeframe, we should be careful to make that kind of optimization for particular scenario, as long as its impact is not justified by detailed analysis. So, straight forward solution as in [3] considering above agreement is preferable.

Proposal 1:

For NR Rel-15, DMRS port ordering is performed as follows for two-CW transmission (DL)

Simple ordering with increasing index (port number)

TB to CW mapping

As described in [5], so far no description captured to support one-CW transmission for the UE configured with higher layer parameter Number-MCS-HARQ-DL-DCI equals 2. That implies mapping of logical channel entity to physical channel is unclear, so corresponding text should be captured in spec. Basically resource allocation framework over spatial domain including MCS/TBS determination is same as that for LTE, therefore it is straight forward to inherit specification text from corresponding description for LTE. Taking this point into account, our proposed modification on TS38.212 and TS38.214 are indicated in appendix part.

Proposal 2:

To apply the text proposals as appendix part.

Conclusion

In this contribution we discuss the codeword mapping related open issues and preferable approaches to complete corresponding specifications.
