Title: Summary of issues on codeword mapping

Document for: Discussion and Decision

Issues on codeword mapping and proposed resolutions

In this contribution, a summary of issues on codeword mapping raised in the seven submitted contributions for RAN1 NR-AH 1801 is given ([1]-[9]). The issues are of two kinds and outlined in Table 1 and 2:

Specification refinement: e.g. clarification

Resolving open issues: e.g. missing components in the specification

Table 1 Specification refinement

| Issue | Summary of proposal | Companies
1.1 | Scrambling sequence initialization | For configured nID, the following holdsapplies only to DCI formats 1_1 and 0_1, otherwise the default is usedIn DCI 1_1, applies for C-RNTI and CS-RNTI otherwise the default is usedIn DCI 0_1, applies for C-RNTI,CS-RNTI and SPS-CSI-RNTI, otherwise the default is used.. | Ericsson
1.2 | On the term “rate matching” | The term “rate matched” is not defined, hence we suggest replacing it with “not available for PDSCH” to align with section 5.1.4 terminology of TS 38.214 | Ericsson

The proposal for resolving issue 1.1 and 1.2 is intended to avoid ambiguities in specification on nID and rate matching, respectively. At this point, whether the proposal for issue 1.1 is needed should be discussed. Issue 1.2, on the other hand, is intended to improve the description on the rate matching operation which would otherwise be ambiguous.

Therefore, the following resolution is possible.

Proposal:

Issue 1.1: discuss further whether specification refinement is needed for nID

Issue 1.2: replace “rate matched” with “not available for PDSCH” to align with section 5.1.4 terminology of TS 38.214

For issue 2.1, the proposal in Alt 2.1.2 is intended to follow up on a previous agreement on DMRS port grouping for rank >4 transmission – primarily intended for multi-TRP/panel applications. On the other hand, since multi-TRP/panel optimization is not a high priority for Phase I, the urgency/necessity of this type of proposal is unclear.

For issue 2.2, reusing the LTE scheme seems reasonable as there is no technical reason for adopting a more complex solution (e.g. switching TB ordering). TB-to-CW mapping is needed to map a logical channel entity to physical channel.

Therefore, the following resolutions are possible.

Proposal:

Issue 2.1: Discuss further, comparing Alt 2.1.1 to 2.1.2

The baseline should be Alt 2.1.1

Issue 2.2: Agree on the proposal in [4] (LTE DCI format 2C approach)
