Additional CW-to-layer correspondence

Before we investigate the additional CW-to-Layer correspondence, let us first revisit the agreed default CW-to-Layer mapping scheme. Specifically, on a per PDSCH basis, at most 2 codewords are allowed. If the number of layer L > 4, the 1st [FIG:68ab56952972bc66b480] layers are mapped to CW0, and the remaining layers are mapped to CW1. In additional, FFS the case when L = 3 or 4, whether NR supports 2 CWs or not. Obviously, the default scheme is pre-defined in specification and therefore simple for both gNB and UE to implement without any need of signaling support. However, it lacks of flexibility in some scenarios, since the CW-to-Layer mapping of LTE-style cannot guarantee to work smoothly in NR scenarios. There are a few of corner cases to which we have to pay attention.

[FIG:94787c5e2cbe90a2db2a]

Two TRPs transmit L = 4 with single CW

Take the multi-TRP/panel transmission in Figure 1 as example. When L = 4, according to the default CW-to-Layer mapping rule, we have only 1 CW, which maps from Layer 1 through Layer 4. The channel quality of first two layers from TRP 1 may significantly different from that of last two layers from TRP 2. Therefore, 2 CQIs reported from UE are more appropriated than single CQI. Otherwise, that single CQI UE measures and reports may be a tradeoff between the layers from TRP 1 and the other layers from TRP 2. It then wastes the strong channel condition of two layers by transmitting with lower MCS level and increases the BLER of the other two layers. Thereby, we have following proposal.

For L = 2/3/4, NR supports additional CW-to-layer correspondence with 2 CWs in Rel.15.

Furthermore, even RAN1 has agreed that there is no support for additional CW-to-layer mapping for >4 layers in Rel.15. However, considering the upcoming progress for multiple-TRP/panel transmission after Dec., 2017 [2], hopefully, NR should at least consider additional CW-to-layer mapping for >4 layers.

Conclusions

Finally, allow us to summary our proposals as below again

For L = 2/3/4, NR supports additional CW-to-layer correspondence with 2 CWs in Rel.15.
